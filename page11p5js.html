#11 - Community Tool or Quiz

Description

This interactive quiz will determine whether the user match the role of a Protagonist, Sidekick, or Villain based on their responses to a series of engaging scenario-based questions. This quiz is designed for individuals who enjoy storytelling, character analysis, and personality-based quizzes. It focuses for gamers, writers, and fans of fiction who are interested in exploring their narrative role in a fun and interactive way.
