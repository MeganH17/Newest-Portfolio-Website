#Explanation for Digital Divitation Project:

Design Process
Inspired by virtual pet games like Pou or Widgetable, this website creates a playful and caring experience where users adopt a virtual cat and learn fun facts every time they interact with it. Each cat is paired with randomized traits and uses the MeowFacts API to deliver a new cat-related fact during every care action. When the user gives affection, in return, they are rewarded with a fun, surprising piece of knowledge.

I grew up loving personality quizzes like MBTI 16 personalities test, Random quizionnare online, or even Tiktok filters that ask you questions. a I’ve also always been obsessed with character roles in stories. Heroes are great, sure, but sidekicks have heart, and villain have flair. I took inspiration from superhero shows, animated series, and comics where your choices shape who you are. I wanted the quiz to feel like a mashup between a self-discovery tool and a mini adventure.

STEP BY STEP: 
The participant chooses to adopt a cat. They either:
Orange Cat
Black Cat
Calico Cat

After choosing the cat player will have to take care of the cat with interations:
Feeding
Playing
Patting 

Every interaction leads to different fun facts such as:
"Cats can jump up to six times their length."
"A group of cats is called a clowder."
"Cats sleep 70% of their lives."
