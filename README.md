# Portfolio Megan New
Design Process
The whole idea started with this question: What kind of character would you be if your life is a fictional story? You can be the main hero, the sidekick, or the villain. I wanted the quiz to feel like a fun quizionnare, not just “pick your favorite color” type stuff, but questions that drop you into little scenes where your choices reveal your personality. I designed it so that even the Villain outcome wouldn’t feel bad, just different. Everyone gets a cool character vibe, no matter what they choose.

Inspiration and References
I grew up loving personality quizzes like MBTI 16 personalities test, Random quizionnare online, or even Tiktok filters that ask you questions. a I’ve also always been obsessed with character roles in stories. Heroes are great, sure, but sidekicks have heart, and villain have flair. I took inspiration from superhero shows, animated series, and comics where your choices shape who you are. I wanted the quiz to feel like a mashup between a self-discovery tool and a mini adventure.

Challenges I Faced
Writing the questions was tough! I didn’t want them to feel boring or super obvious, like “Are you good or evil?” Instead, I wanted every question to feel like part of a story. I want to make sure each answer path led somewhere cool. Incorporating Java Script was also a challange because it was my first time, but I use it for the game logic and also some of the animation.
